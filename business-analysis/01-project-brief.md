# Hotel Booking Cancellation Process Improvement

## Project Status

Independent business analysis case study based on a public hotel booking dataset. The business scenario, stakeholder needs and proposed process changes are simulated for portfolio purposes. The historical findings are derived from the accompanying Tableau analysis.

## Business Context

A hotel group operating a city hotel and a resort hotel is experiencing a high volume of booking cancellations. Management is concerned that cancellations reduce the reliability of occupancy forecasts, increase operational uncertainty and expose potential booking revenue to risk.

The existing dashboard analyses 119,390 historical bookings and identifies substantial differences in cancellation behaviour across hotels, lead-time groups, market segments and booking characteristics. The organisation now needs to determine how these findings could support an improved reservation-management process.

## Problem Statement

The organisation does not currently have a structured, risk-based process for identifying reservations that may require confirmation or follow-up. Reservation staff may therefore apply the same process to bookings with very different cancellation characteristics, while management has limited visibility of where intervention would provide the greatest value.

Historical analysis shows an overall cancellation rate of 37.04%. However, the data identifies associations rather than confirmed causes. Further stakeholder investigation and operational validation would be required before implementing any policy or system change.

## Project Objectives

- Understand the current reservation and cancellation-management process.
- Identify the stakeholders affected by proposed process changes.
- Examine cancellation patterns and potential operational pain points.
- Define an improved future-state reservation-monitoring process.
- Establish business, functional and non-functional requirements.
- Prioritise requirements according to business value and implementation need.
- Define user stories, acceptance criteria and UAT scenarios.
- Recommend KPIs for measuring the success of a pilot implementation.

## In Scope

- Reservation monitoring after a booking has been created.
- Identification of bookings that may require confirmation or follow-up.
- Staff visibility of cancellation risk indicators.
- Customer confirmation reminders.
- Management reporting on cancellations and potential booking-value exposure.
- Business requirements for a proposed reservation-monitoring solution.
- Pilot implementation and performance measurement.

## Out of Scope

- Development of a production prediction model.
- Automatic rejection or cancellation of customer bookings.
- Replacement of the full property-management system.
- Final decisions on deposits, cancellation fees or contractual policies.
- Confirmed financial-loss calculations.
- Legal interpretation of customer-contract or data-protection obligations.
- Implementation of the proposed solution in a real hotel.

## Key Constraints

- The data covers arrivals from July 2015 to August 2017.
- The hotels and customers are anonymised.
- No interviews with actual hotel stakeholders were conducted.
- The dataset does not capture every operational or behavioural cause of cancellation.
- Potential revenue at risk is an analytical proxy rather than confirmed lost revenue.
- Recommendations must be validated through stakeholder consultation and a controlled pilot.
